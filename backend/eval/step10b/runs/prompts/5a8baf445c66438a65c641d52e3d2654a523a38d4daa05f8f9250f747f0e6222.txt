Question: How many different customers whose current address is in California were billed for catalog purchases made in 2002?

Choice 1: date_dim can be joined in these ways:
  A. catalog_sales.cs_sold_date_sk = date_dim.d_date_sk — Each date dimension row has many catalog sales rows, through their sold date surrogate key.
  B. customer.c_first_shipto_date_sk = date_dim.d_date_sk — Each date dimension row has many customer rows, through their first ship-to date surrogate key.
  C. customer.c_last_review_date_sk = date_dim.d_date_sk — Each date dimension row has many customer rows, through their last review date surrogate key (asserted in the overlay, not declared by the database).
  D. customer.c_first_sales_date_sk = date_dim.d_date_sk — Each date dimension row has many customer rows, through their first sales date surrogate key.
